How to read the archive

Snapshot compiled October 3, 2026. The in-progress 2026 season is excluded.

PCT changes meaning across the archive

RTSports displays 25-13-1 as .641 in 2013 (wins ÷ games), but 30-7-2 as .795 in 2016 and 28-10-1 as .731 in 2017 (wins plus half a tie ÷ games). Season-table PCT values are reproduced exactly. All-time win percentage uses the standard (W + T/2) ÷ games formula for a fair cross-era comparison.

Verified coverage

  • All six season-summary fields for every season from 2011–2025.
  • All 14 teams' W/L/T, PCT, PF, PA and Total Points for each of 15 seasons.
  • All 429 picks from the June 5, 2011 startup auction.
  • Franchise lineages, owner boundaries and current identities.

Archive conventions

  • Standings remain in the archive's display order; finish is that table position.
  • PF and PA are season totals exactly as displayed.
  • Total Points (PTS) is the archive's season-long points column.
  • Highest single week is 282 by Ghost of Derrick Thomas in 2020 NFL Week 12. Each season’s final playoff week is excluded because its displayed score already includes the prior week in the two-week aggregate final.
  • Each franchise played 39 games per season: every opponent three times.

Commissioner-completed 2024–25 records

  • 2024 final: GoodFellas 375–285 over Pestilence; third: He Hate Covid 454–433 over Burd.
  • 2024 Loser Bowl: Scalp Hunter 380–377 over Cuzzo.
  • 2025 final: GoodFellas 471–417 over Magoo; third: Burd 426–383 over He Hate Covid.
  • 2025 Loser Bowl: Bird Gang 364–241 over Pepe Silvia, reflecting the commissioner's explicit decision.

League origin

  • Founded in 2010 with 16 teams; no 2010 season was played.
  • Five founders folded before the 2011 auction: Renegades of Sack, Team Frei, Team Heist, Team Lucia and Team Quinlan.
  • Every played season in this archive had 14 teams.
